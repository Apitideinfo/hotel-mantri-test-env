User Data Deletion & Privacy Instructions

In accordance with Meta Platform policies, the Indian Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act), and international privacy standards, this page explains how hotel guests, staff, and WhatsApp recipients can request deletion of personal information.

How Data Deletion Works at HotelMantri

Step 1

Submit Request

Provide your contact email/phone, relationship (guest, staff, or WhatsApp recipient), and details via the form below or email privacy@hotelmantri.com.

Step 2

Identity Verification

Our compliance desk confirms the request originates from the actual data subject to prevent malicious third-party deletions.

Step 3

Purge & Confirmation

Non-financial PII is permanently erased or pseudonymized within 30 days, and an auditable confirmation record is maintained.

Statutory Tax & Accounting Retention Exceptions

Under Section 44AA of the Indian Income Tax Act and Section 36 of the CGST Act, hotel establishments and processors are legally required to preserve accounting books and tax invoices for 6 to 8 years. Financial invoice numbers and transaction ledgers are restricted under statutory retention, while contact numbers and personal marketing details are permanently scrubbed.
