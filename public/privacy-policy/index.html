Privacy Policy

HotelMantri is committed to transparent, secure, and compliant data stewardship. This policy explains how we collect, process, safeguard, and retain information across our cloud PMS platform, guest communication channels, and Meta/WhatsApp integrations.

Effective Date: October 15, 2026 Version: 3.2 (Production Release) Canonical URL: https://www.hotelmantri.com/privacy-policy
1

Introduction and Scope

HotelMantri (operated under Apitide Information Technologies) is a comprehensive cloud-native hospitality management platform. Our services include Property Management System (PMS) operations, Point of Sale (POS) dining operations, Channel Manager integration, Direct Booking Engine, GST invoicing, financial reporting, and automated guest messaging notifications.

This Privacy Policy applies to all interactions with the HotelMantri website (www.hotelmantri.com), the HotelMantri web software application, authorized mobile interfaces, APIs, and integrated communications channels such as WhatsApp and transactional email.

Key Legal Distinctions: Roles in Data Processing

Under the Indian Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act), GDPR, and international privacy frameworks:

  • Hotel Operator as Data Controller: The hotel, resort, or property owner using HotelMantri determines what guest records are required for check-in, identity verification, local police verification (Form C in India), and stay arrangements.
  • HotelMantri as Data Processor: HotelMantri acts strictly as a technology service provider and Data Processor. We store, transmit, and organize guest information solely on behalf of, and under explicit instruction from, the hotel operator.
2

Information Collected

Depending on your interactions with HotelMantri, we process the following genuine categories of personal and operational information:

Guest & Reservation Data Full legal name, phone number, email address, physical address, city, state, postal code, nationality, government ID proof type (Passport, Driving License, Voter ID, Aadhaar) and verified masked ID number, check-in/out dates, room assignments, guest count, and booking source.
Hotel & Staff Accounts Property business name, legal entity name, GSTIN tax ID, official address, owner contact phone/email, staff employee usernames, assigned role permissions (Admin, Manager, Reception, Housekeeping, Accounts), and shift logs.
Financial & Invoice Records Room tariffs, GST tax splits (CGST, SGST, IGST), payment mode (Cash, UPI, Card POS, Bank Transfer, Online Payment Gateway), payment transaction references, advances, and balances. Note: HotelMantri does not collect or store raw 16-digit credit card numbers or CVVs.
WhatsApp, Meta & Technical Data Guest mobile numbers used for automated booking alerts, message delivery timestamps and statuses (sent, delivered, read, failed), server access logs, IP addresses (for rate limiting and DDoS defense), and request correlation IDs (X-Request-Id).
3

How Information is Collected

  • Direct Input by Hotel Front Office Staff: During check-in, walk-in reservations, staff management, and daily ledger entry.
  • Direct Guest Interactions: When guests complete pre-check-in forms, interact with direct booking links, make payments, or request digital invoices.
  • Online Travel Agencies (OTAs): Synchronized automatically via our Channel Manager integration (Aiosell, Channex, Booking.com, MakeMyTrip, Agoda, Expedia) when a traveler books a room online.
  • Automated Telemetry: Session cookies and security telemetry captured to maintain authentication and secure multi-tenant isolation.
  • Messaging Webhooks: Webhook delivery receipts from the Meta WhatsApp Cloud API / Whinta platform confirming delivery of service slips.
4

Purposes of Data Processing

  • Front-Office Operations: Room allocation, check-ins, check-outs, folios, and housekeeping schedules.
  • Statutory Invoicing: Generating GST tax invoices, calculating tax splits, and preparing daily reconciliation registers.
  • Transactional Notifications: Sending automated booking confirmation cards, check-in instructions, digital invoice PDF links, and daily manager reports over WhatsApp.
  • Platform Security: Enforcing PostgreSQL Row-Level Security, multi-tenant isolation, and defending against automated brute-force attacks.
5

Hotel Reservation and Guest Information Processing

HotelMantri processes guest information exclusively as a technology processor under contract with the respective hotel operator. Guest data is segregated inside each hotel's secure partition. HotelMantri never sells, trades, or discloses guest lists to advertising networks.

Guests seeking to view, correct, or delete reservation records should contact the hotel where they stayed, or email our compliance desk at privacy@hotelmantri.com.

WhatsApp and Meta Integrations

HotelMantri integrates with Meta Platforms, Inc. technologies, including the WhatsApp Business Cloud API (operated with Business Solution Provider Whinta).

Strictly Transactional Utility Messaging
  • WhatsApp messaging is utilized solely for service-related notifications requested by the user (booking slips, invoice links, daily reports).
  • No spam, cold broadcasts, or unsolicited marketing campaigns.
  • All outbound automated templates are pre-approved by Meta under WhatsApp Business Messaging Policies.
  • Opt-out: Recipients can reply STOP to any message or submit an opt-out ticket at https://www.hotelmantri.com/data-deletion.

Storage, Security, and Access Controls

  • End-to-End Encryption: Modern TLS 1.2/1.3 with HTTPS enforced across all endpoints. AES-256 encryption at rest for databases.
  • PostgreSQL Row-Level Security: Strict multi-tenant isolation ensuring properties cannot access another property's records.
  • Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Granular permissions restricting staff access according to job responsibilities.
  • Rate Limiting: Automated DDoS and brute-force defenses on authentication and payment endpoints.
9

Data Retention

Records are retained only for as long as necessary for operational, legal, and tax compliance:

  • Active Accounts: Maintained for the duration of the hotel's active subscription.
  • Statutory Invoices & Ledgers: Under Section 44AA of the Indian Income Tax Act and Section 36 of the CGST Act, financial records and tax invoices must be preserved for 6 to 8 years. Financial transactions cannot be erased prematurely.
  • Non-Financial Contact PII: Purged or anonymized upon validated request.
  • Server Access Logs: Rotated and deleted within 30 to 90 days.
